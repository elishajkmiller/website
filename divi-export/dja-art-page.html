4th & Main

Andrew Batcheller

Kansas City native and professional oil painter who lives and operates a studio in Joplin, Missouri. Batcheller's distinct, ultra-surrealist and sometimes macabre artwork focuses heavily on “portraiture” that uses birds as proxies or analogies for the human condition, inviting deep contemplation regarding human drama, social consciousness, climate change, and the urge to persevere. Known for his incredible attention to detail, smooth gradients, and turbulent ocean backdrops, his work has been acquired by both national and international art collectors. “The World owes us nothing. We owe each other the World.”

John Brand

John Brand is a nature and wildlife photographer based in the Lake of the Ozarks area of Missouri. His work spans landscapes, botanicals, and wildlife, drawn to subjects that reveal unexpected detail and color in the natural world. His image Dust to Dust was captured during a summer morning in his backyard, where a bumblebee covered in pollen works the center of a Gaillardia in full bloom.

Robyn Brower

Minnesota artist Robyn Brower works in chalk pastel, creating detailed drawings inspired by close observation of the natural world. Her work explores landscapes, gardens, and the intricate relationships between plants and insects. Mike Byrne

Artist Mike Byrne takes a minimalist approach, using everyday objects in unexpected ways to celebrate the often-overlooked artistry of industrial design. While preserving each object's original purpose, he adds intellectually playful titles that invite viewers to make new connections. His goal is to spark an emotional response, especially that amusing “aha!” moment when a viewer sees something familiar in a whole new way.

Jacqueline Claudet Mitterer

Born in Lima, Peru in 1971, Jackie C. Mitterer pioneers a distinctive “Modern 3DMosaic” technique, infusing traditional methods with a painter's perspective. Intuitively layering sea glass, ceramics, and stone, her textured, three-dimensional works transcend flat compositions. This innovative approach redefines the medium, inviting dynamic viewer engagement and establishing her as a trailblazer in mosaic art.

Mary Jane Mooneyham

Mary Jane Mooneyham is an award-winning multidisciplinary artist whose creative journey began with crayons and evolved through oils and acrylics, ultimately leading her to the expressive beauty of watercolor. Her work is inspired by the quiet poetry of everyday moments, fleeting experiences, and tender expressions that evoke a sense of shared emotion. Portraiture, landscapes, urbanscapes, flora, and fauna—along with her distinctive 3D watercolor paper sculptures—become subjects through which she explores light, color, atmosphere, and connection. Mooneyham’s work celebrates the beauty found in both the extraordinary and the seemingly ordinary world around us.

Vlad Zadneprianski

Vlad Zadneprianski was born and raised in Ukraine, moved to USA in the late 90s and is an architect living and working in New York City. For decades Vlad has experimented with what he calls “Compositionism” - semi-abstract art based on interaction and communication between simple geometric forms where composition plays a major role. Minimalist in nature and being closer to avant-garde side of art Vlad uses composition tools not only to illustrate, but rather to express. Vlad also has been drawing cartoons since he was a child illustrating life as he sees it, covering everything from everyday situations all the way to politics. Over the years Vlad published his work in newspapers worldwide, participated in exhibitions, art installations, and created numerous illustrations, greeting cards and murals.
