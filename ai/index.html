AI policy

I run AI only on local models, on computers I own in Joplin, MO.

  • Where it runs. I use open models on my own hardware. I do not use hosted AI services, data centers or rented servers (VPS).
  • Your data. Personal data stays on that hardware and is never given to any outside individual or company.
  • Retention. I delete personal data once it is no longer needed. The exception is names and contact details, which I keep so we can stay in touch.
