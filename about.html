About me

I'm Elisha. I build things, mostly software, and some things with a soldering iron.

I started as a direct support professional at a nonprofit in Missouri that supports people with intellectual and developmental disabilities. Now I'm the only developer there. I go around the different departments and build tools that help people do their jobs. The software this field is stuck with is expensive and built around billing, not around the people doing the work. So I'm building Robin, an open-source record system for human services nonprofits. It's the most real thing I've made.

On my own time I make hardware and firmware. I wrote a UI for the LilyGO T-Deck Pro on top of Meshtastic, and firmware for a Watchy that gets my texts from it. I'm also converting a kei truck to electric with a battery I'm building from scratch.

I like looking at how things on the internet actually behave. I read headers, requests and DNS records, then write down what I find. A couple of those write-ups are on the blog.

I run the website and operations for POW417, a 48-hour human-only filmmaking competition in Springfield, MO. No AI-generated content. Forty-something people show up, make something in two days, and it means something because people made it.

The rest is the Fediverse, self-hosting everything I can, and music in REAPER on Linux Mint.

I don't have a degree. I learned by building things for people I know.
