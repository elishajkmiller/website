Basics of caring for one

A dog is a commitment of a decade or more. These four things keep them happy and healthy.

1

Food & water

Quality food sized to their breed and age. Fresh water available at all times.

2

Exercise & play

Daily walks and a chance to run. Bored dogs get into things.

3

Vet & vaccines

Regular checkups, vaccinations, and parasite prevention keep them safe.

4

Training & love

Consistent, positive training plus plain old attention. That's the whole recipe.

Quick facts

A few things worth knowing.

  • • A dog's nose is nearly as unique as a human's fingerprint.
  • • They can smell roughly 100,000 times better than people.
  • • Dogs dream, just like we do — twitching and soft whimpers are a giveaway.
  • • A dog's "smile" (the relaxed open mouth) is a genuine happiness signal.
  • • They can recognize their own name — and the names of about 25 other words.
