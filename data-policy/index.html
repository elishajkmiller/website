Data policy

Personal data

  • Where it stays. Personal data stays on hardware I own, in Joplin, MO.
  • Sharing. It is never given to any outside individual or company.
  • Retention. I delete personal data within 30 days after a project ends, and from backups within 90 days. I keep names and contact details so we can stay in touch, and business records, such as invoices, for as long as the law requires. I will delete your personal data sooner if you ask.

Local Open AI

I run open, local AI on my personal computers.

  • Where it runs. I use open models on my own hardware. I do not use hosted AI services, data centers or rented servers (VPS).
