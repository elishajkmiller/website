48 hours.
No prompt.
Make a film.

Hour 00 · KickoffOpening ceremonies

Gather with your crew. The clock starts when ceremonies close. No prompt, just your vision, your team, and 48 hours.

Hours 01 to 12 · Pre-proWrite and prep

Develop your concept, write your script, scout locations, cast your crew. This is where your film starts to exist.

Hours 12 to 36 · ProductionShoot it

Roll camera. Everything is practical: real locations, real light, real performances. No AI-generated anything.

Hours 36 to 48 · PostCut and deliver

Edit, mix, color, and deliver. Human hands on every frame. Submission is final. The clock is the great equalizer.

The showcase · Date TBAWe watch together

Every film screens publicly at the showcase. Every crew gets seen. No winners, no losers, just filmmakers in the same room.

Not a competition.
A coming together.

POW417 is a 48-hour film gathering built around one simple idea: what happens when people actually show up and make something real, together, by hand?

No rankings. No trophies. No algorithm. Just crews, cameras, and the clock. Every team gets the same deadline and the same constraints. What you do with it is entirely yours.

We built this because the 417 deserves a film event that respects the craft. One that says the human eye, the human hand, and the human story still matter, maybe more than ever.

The only rules that matter

These aren't restrictions. They're the whole point.

01
No AI. Not even a little.Non-negotiable

No AI-generated images, voice, scripts, music, visual effects, or any tool that uses generative AI in any part of your pipeline.

02
All footage shot in the 48Hard rule

Every frame of your film must be captured during the official 48-hour window. Stock footage is not permitted.

03
5 minutes maximumCore

Your finished film, credits included, must not exceed 5 minutes.

04
Show up for the screeningExpected

This isn't a drop-box event. Being present at the screening is part of the deal.
